# Survey on World Models

## Introduction
World models are representations that aim to capture the dynamics of environments, allowing agents to anticipate responses to their actions. This report surveys the concept of world models, their utilization in various domains, and the associated challenges.

## What is a World Model?
A world model is a system that creates an internal depiction of the environment, enabling prediction and planning without the need for extensive real-world trials. Typical applications span robotics, autonomous vehicles, and interactive systems. They leverage techniques such as joint embedding predictive architecture (JEPA) for state predictions, showcasing minimal reliance on physical testing [1].

## Utilization in Artificial Intelligence
World models are pivotal for enhancing planning and decision-making capabilities [2]. They are employed widely within domains such as robotics [3], where they facilitate anticipated reactions to actions and foster autonomous driving through comprehensive simulations encompassing even rare scenarios [4].

## Types of World Models
World models can be categorized based on their functionality and application domains [5]:
1. **Latent Prediction Models** - Represent the environment through latent variables and predict outcomes based on these abstractions. 
2. **Generative Models** - Capable of generating visual outputs based on learned interactions and states, instrumental in areas like video synthesis.
3. **3D Spatial Models** - Often used in robotics for physical interaction and manipulation, these models mimic real-world physics to predict object interactions and movements in three-dimensional space [6].

## Challenges in Creation and Employment
Despite their capabilities, challenges persist in developing accurate and robust world models [7]. Key issues include:
- **Data Scarcity**: There is a limited availability of rich action datasets necessary for training the models effectively [8].
- **Generalization**: Many models struggle to generalize across environments, often requiring regular updates to maintain predictive accuracy [9].
- **Safety and Trust**: Ensuring the reliability of predictions in unknown environments remains a focal concern, particularly for applications in safety-critical domains.

## Conclusion
World models represent a sophisticated means to navigate and manipulate complex environments through predictive modeling. While significant advancements have been made, ongoing research addresses the challenges of data quality, generalization, and safety, essential for the models' application across various sectors.
